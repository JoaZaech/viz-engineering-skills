Domain

  • P1Stakeholder: the club's ride organizer (S1), planning the 2027 calendar and sharing one chart in the newsletter.
  • P2Question: Q1, which months give the most good riding days.
  • P3Tone: practical and plain; headlines say what a rider can expect.

Data

  • P4Good riding day = 0 mm rain and a high of 15–30 °C.
  • P5Derived outcome per day: Good, Wet (any rain), Too cold (dry, high < 15 °C), Too hot (dry, high > 30 °C). Wet wins over temperature.
  • P6Months are compared on the average of 2012–2015 (good days ÷ 4); single years only in design 4.
  • P7Key numbers: May 20.8, Jun 19.0, Jul 22.1, Aug 21.1, Sep 20.5 good days per month; Dec 0, Jan 0.5, Nov 0.5. In July 23 of 124 days are too hot and 11 wet.

Task

  • P8Main task: {compare, distribution} of good days across months, to pick the core season.
  • P9Secondary tasks: {identify, features} why a month fails (rain, cold or heat); {compare, trends} between years for reliability.

Encoding

  • P11Labels on the marks wherever they fit; a legend only where four outcomes share a mark.
